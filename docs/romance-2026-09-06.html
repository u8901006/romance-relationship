MDMA 輔助認知行為夫妻療法對創傷後壓力症狀軍人及伴侶的初步試驗

Journal of traumatic stress · Healing together: Results from a pilot trial of 3,4-Methylenedioxymethamphetamine-enhanced cognitive-behavioral conjoint therapy for posttraumatic stress disorder.

#5 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 中實用性

Journal of personality and social psychology · The family environment in late childhood and early adolescence as a predictor of narcissism in emerging adulthood: Findings from a 17-year longitudinal study of Mexican-origin youth.

長期追蹤發現，童年中期家庭溫暖與親子關係品質正向預測成年期自戀的傲慢與優越感，而敵對與衝突則預測剝削性與脆弱自戀，但此效應多經青少年期自戀中介。

#7 ⏳ 低實用性

法國中老年男女之 sexually active life expectancy 性別差異研究

Perspectives on sexual and reproductive health · Gender Differences in Sexually Active Life Expectancy in France.

男性 20-24 歲時 sexually active life expectancy 為 47.1 年，女性 44.5 年；納入自慰後差距縮小，而納入性滿意度後男性因不滿意損失 11 年，女性損失 8 年，顯示品質對壽命指標之調整效應。
